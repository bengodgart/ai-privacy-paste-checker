AI Privacy Paste Checker does one thing: check your message before you send it to an AI chat. That is the whole v1, on purpose.

A couple directions it could go, if it turns out to be useful to more than just me:

A small snippet a business could paste next to its internal "how to use AI responsibly" policy, so the check shows up right where people are about to paste something, not in a separate tab they never open.

A wider list of health and financial words. Right now it knows 12 curated terms. Real messages use more ways to say the same sensitive things than any list can fully cover.

None of that is committed. If you paste something into it and it misses something obvious, or flags something that clearly is not sensitive, that is the most useful thing you could tell me.
